The model should not come before the question.

A sophisticated model can still be useless if it solves the wrong problem. We begin by understanding the domain, the data and the outcome that matters.

Understand the domainWhat does the data actually represent?
Define the real problemWhat decision, outcome or discovery matters?
Choose the right methodAnalytics, statistics, Machine Learning or AI — only when appropriate.
Validate rigorouslyPerformance, assumptions, reliability and business relevance all matter.

Research Vision

Beyond commercial analytics.

Pratap Labs is being built with a long-term interdisciplinary ambition: applying computation, Data Science and AI to difficult questions about life, mind and human well-being — connecting business, brain, cell and the science of happiness under one research direction.

Brain & Cognition

Exploring how Data Science, Machine Learning and AI can contribute to understanding brain activity, cognition, behaviour and intelligence.

Cell & Biological Systems

Investigating computational approaches to complex cellular and biological data, patterns and systems — from the cellular level to broader questions about human life.

Happiness Index & Well-being

Building richer, evidence-based, interdisciplinary ways — spanning data, psychology, neuroscience, biology and society — to understand and quantify human flourishing and quality of life.

Perspective

Nuance matters.

The value of Data Science lies not only in model performance, but in the quality of the question, the validity of the evidence and the usefulness of the decision that follows.

MODEL STRATEGY

When the “better” model is not the better business choice

Performance, interpretability, implementation cost, risk and decision context must be considered together.

AI VALIDATION

AI can accelerate analysis without becoming the authority

Generated answers, code and reasoning still require independent validation and professional judgment.

BUSINESS ANALYTICS

The most valuable analysis may begin by reframing the problem

Understanding the decision behind the data often matters more than selecting the algorithm.
