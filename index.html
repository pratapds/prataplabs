How we think

From a headline metric to a better business question.

An illustrative retail scenario demonstrates how a business question becomes an analytical investigation and a decision framework.

Illustrative question

Sales are increasing, but is the business becoming more profitable?

Revenue can hide shifts in discounting, customer mix, product economics and fulfilment cost. Good analysis separates these drivers before drawing conclusions.

Illustrative approach

Investigate → Compare → Prioritise

Compare sales and margin by segment, study changes over time, test plausible explanations, then recommend which actions merit validation.

Illustrative scenario only. It is not a client engagement or a claim of achieved results.

Questions

Before you write to us.

How do we communicate?

All enquiries, scoping, proposals, project correspondence and delivery are handled in writing by email. We do not arrange phone or video meetings.

What kinds of organisations do you work with?

We work with organisations facing meaningful business decisions where strategy, analysis or practical AI can add value. The suitability of an engagement depends on the problem, available information and scope—not a fixed employee count.

What do you need to start?

A short description of the business, the decision you face and what information exists. Please do not send client or personal data with the first email; handling is agreed in writing first.

How are fees set?

Fees are proposed in writing after the scope is clear, so you can review deliverables, timing and terms before committing. There is no obligation from an enquiry.

How do you approach outcomes and uncertainty?

Findings depend on the available evidence, business context and implementation. We explain assumptions, limitations and trade-offs rather than guarantee a particular commercial result.

Do you always recommend AI?

No. AI is recommended only where it solves a defined problem better than simpler methods. Often a clearer metric or process change is the better answer.
